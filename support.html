Support

Answers to common questions are below. Tap a question to open it.

Is this a prediction or medical advice?

No. Extra Mornings shows population averages for education only. It is not a prediction about you and not medical advice. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Where do the numbers come from?

The Social Security Administration's Actuarial Life Table (Period Life Table 2023, as used in the 2026 Trustees Report), cross-checked with CDC/NCHS United States Life Tables 2023, plus peer-reviewed studies on activity, smoking and sleep. Every source is listed on the Sources screen in the app.

Why can't I add the habit gains together?

Each figure comes from a separate study, and the effects overlap, so they can't simply be added.

What does the share card show?

Only a count of extra mornings. It never shows your age, birthdate, sex or habits.

I bought it on another phone. Do I pay again?

No. Download it again from the App Store with the same Apple Account at no charge.

Privacy

Data Not Collected

Extra Mornings works fully offline. Your answers are stored only on your iPhone and never leave it. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and makes no network requests.
